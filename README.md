# Curveshot

Live Demo: [https://rencheng0525.github.io/Curveshot/](https://rencheng0525.github.io/Curveshot/)

Generated by Claude with prompts only, no manual code editing.

> 🌌 A bullet-hell shooter where every bullet falls through curved spacetime. Inspired by classic formation shooters like *Galaga* and warping-grid shooters, with a quiet tribute to general relativity and to **GW150914**, the first gravitational wave ever detected by LIGO.

## Overview

A web-based **3D arcade shooter** played on a living grid of spacetime. Every ship, missile and bullet has mass and presses a well into the grid beneath it. Gravity bends bullets, clocks run slow near heavy objects, and you can turn time itself backward. Built with **Three.js (WebGL)**, with music and sound effects synthesized in real time — no image or audio files.

## Features

### 🕸️ A Grid That Feels Gravity

- **Mass Wells**: Everything with mass dents the grid, and the dents follow their owners.
- **Shockwaves**: Explosions send ripples rolling across the surface.
- **Scrolling Space**: The grid flows toward you during flight, slows to a crawl in boss fights and surges between stages.

### 🌀 General Relativity, Played

- **Bent Bullets**: Bullets curve around heavy bosses and carriers. All bullets bend equally regardless of size, a nod to the equivalence principle.
- **Time Dilation**: Clocks tick at the Schwarzschild rate, √(1 − rs/r). Bullets slow down near deep wells, and so does your ship.
- **Gravitational Redshift**: Anything deep in a well shifts toward red and dims, then recovers as it climbs out.
- **Two Clocks**: The *Ship clock* shows your proper time; the *Far clock* shows the time of a distant observer. Watch them drift apart.
- **Binary Black Hole**: The final boss is a pair of black holes of 36 and 29 solar masses, spiralling inward under Kepler's law until they merge, radiating quadrupole gravitational waves with a rising chirp.

### ⏪ Time Rewind

- **Hold to Rewind**: The whole world plays backward — bullets fly back into their guns, explosions un-happen, the grid scrolls in reverse.
- **Undo Death**: Rewind right after being shot down to get your life back, even from the edge of game over.
- **Closed Timelike Curve**: While rewinding, the far clock runs backward but your ship clock keeps moving forward.
- **Limited**: Up to 3 seconds of charge, refilled slowly; history resets at every stage.

### 🛡️ Weapons and Abilities

- **Twin Cannon**: Fires automatically. Yellow gems upgrade it to 4 and then 6 streams.
- **Homing Missiles**: Launch automatically, peel out to the sides and curve onto the nearest target. Green gems add more.
- **Anti-Gravity Shield**: Raises a glowing hill in the grid that pushes enemy bullets away. Slows you down and drains energy.
- **Black-Hole Bomb**: Opens a black hole ahead of you that swallows bullets, destroys missiles and damages everything nearby.

### 🤖 AI Pilot

- Scores 17 candidate moves every frame by predicting where every bullet, missile and enemy will be over the next half second.
- Balances survival against lining up under a target, collecting gems and keeping a comfortable distance.
- Raises the shield when danger climbs, drops a bomb when a hit looks unavoidable, and rewinds after being shot down.
- With the AI on, the game keeps going forever: it continues to the next loop after the ending and restarts after a game over — perfect as a demo.

## 🛠 Technical Highlights

- **Three.js (WebGL)** with UnrealBloom post-processing and OrbitControls.
- **Spring-mass grid** of about 3,500 vertices, each pulled by every nearby mass and shockwave.
- **InstancedMesh** renders up to 1,100 bullets in a single draw call, each with its own redshifted color.
- **Gravitational waves** use a retarded-time ring buffer, so wavefronts travel outward at a finite speed and form spirals.
- **Snapshot rewind**: the whole world is captured 30 times a second, and entities are restored in place so every reference stays valid.
- **Web Audio API** for a synthesized soundtrack that follows the game (menu, battle, boss, ending, rewind) and all sound effects — zero audio files.
- **Full-window layout** that adapts to landscape and portrait screens, respecting phone notches.
- **Feature flags** at the top of the file (`gravityWells`, `gridScroll`, `rewind`).
- **Single HTML file** — no build step. Three.js is loaded from the jsDelivr CDN, so an internet connection is required.

Practice any stage directly by adding `#stage=1` to `#stage=7` to the URL.

## 🌐 Browser Support
